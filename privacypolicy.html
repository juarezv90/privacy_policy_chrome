Privacy Policy

PMG Athena Extension · Last updated: October 7, 2026

1 Information We Access

This extension accesses page content on *.athenahealth.com solely to identify specific elements (such as appointment rows) based on their text content and apply user-configured CSS styles.

It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, patient data, protected health information (PHI), or browsing history.

2 How We Use Information

The accessed page content is processed locally in your browser to apply visual modifications. No page content, text, or metadata is transmitted to our servers or any third party.

Any user preferences (such as style rules) are stored locally using the browser's built-in storage API and never leave your device.

3 Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise transfer any user data to outside parties. The extension operates entirely on your device.

4 Limited Use

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5 Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
